name: idea-architecture
description: Turn literature and a vague research direction into ranked paper ideas, novelty checks, a conference-style architecture, and a non-executing experiment plan.
---

Use the installed ARS skills `academic-research-skills:source-command-ars-lit-review`, `academic-research-skills:source-command-ars-plan`, `academic-research-skills:source-command-ars-outline`, and `academic-research-skills:source-command-ars-reviewer` together with the installed ARIS skills `aris:research-lit`, `aris:idea-creator`, `aris:novelty-check`, `aris:research-review`, `aris:research-refine`, `aris:experiment-plan`, and `aris:research-wiki`. Keep the vendor prefix in handoff notes so same-named skills cannot be confused. Suggested sequence: ARS literature and problem framing; ARIS candidate generation; ARIS novelty and review; ARIS method refinement; ARIS `experiment-plan`. Read the Wiki before ideation and write back accepted and rejected ideas with reasons. The experiment plan may specify baselines, metrics, ablations, datasets, fuzzing targets, run order, and expected result patterns. Never execute code, launch fuzzers, deploy GPUs, monitor runs, or invent experimental results.
