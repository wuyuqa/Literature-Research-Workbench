---
name: manuscript-writing
description: Write and revise a computer-security manuscript in LaTeX using ARIS-style research writing and user-provided evidence.
---

Use ARIS `paper-writing` as the primary writing workflow and `rebuttal` for reviewer responses. Use `latex-compile` and `latex-doctor` for LaTeX validation. Do not use Nature-style writing skills by default. Read the approved idea report, experiment plan, literature reports, and user-provided experiment results. Never fabricate results, tables, metrics, citations, or implementation details. Keep claims traceable to a source or an explicitly supplied result. Produce editable `.tex`, `.bib`, figure references, a change log, and a list of unresolved evidence gaps. Do not run experiments or alter source data.
