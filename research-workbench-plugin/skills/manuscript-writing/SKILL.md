---
name: manuscript-writing
description: Write and revise a computer-security manuscript in LaTeX using ARIS-style research writing and user-provided evidence.
---

Use ARIS `aris:paper-plan`, `aris:paper-write`, `aris:paper-compile`, and `aris:rebuttal` as the primary writing workflow. For verification, use `aris:citation-audit` and `aris:paper-claim-audit`, then `latex-compile` and `latex-doctor` for Codex-side LaTeX validation. Do not use Nature-style writing skills by default. Read the approved idea report, experiment plan, literature reports, and user-provided experiment results. Never fabricate results, tables, metrics, citations, or implementation details. Keep claims traceable to a source or an explicitly supplied result. Produce editable `.tex`, `.bib`, figure references, a change log, and a list of unresolved evidence gaps. Do not run experiments or alter source data.
